Section Five

Mastro's reading

Six conclusions the data supports, with the confidence each one earns.

High confidence

Your restaurant bill is a frequency problem, not an indulgence problem

$8,944 across 190 visits averages just $47 a ticket. 119 of those tickets were under $50 and only six cleared $150. There is no blowout dinner in this data — there are 27 ordinary meals a month, at Neptune's Grill 18 times, Mademoiselle Paris 10 times, the Sourdough Co. 9 times.

Recommendation Cutting spending here means removing visits, not ordering differently. Dropping six meals a month at your own average ticket returns roughly $280/month, or $3,400 a year — with no change to how you eat when you do go out.
High confidence

March was not a bad month — it was four unrelated bills landing in one cycle

March hit $8,503, 73% above the $4,901 January–February baseline. But $3,449 of the overage came from four charges that share nothing: a $1,294 dental procedure, a $953 hotel, a $626 A/C repair, and $576 in United flights. None of them recur anywhere else in the seven months.

Recommendation Do not treat March as a signal of drift. Treat it as evidence that your true exposure is convergence risk — a sinking fund of ~$400/month would have absorbed all four without a spike.
Strong signal

Sixteen percent of your transactions decide fifty-eight percent of your money

94 charges of $100 or more account for $23,227. Meanwhile the 251 charges under $25 — 43% of every line on the statement — total only $3,199, or 8% of the year. The small transactions generate almost all of the mental friction and almost none of the cost.

Recommendation Stop auditing coffee and parking. Put a single review step in front of any charge above $100 — that is about thirteen decisions a month, and it is where the entire budget actually lives.
Moderate confidence

Your AI spend isn't a subscription problem — it's five impulse purchases

The recurring AI floor is only $40 a month: Runway $15, OpenAI $20, ElevenLabs $5, flat from February through June. Yet the category cost $1,553. The gap is five one-off charges — a $350 Runway credit buy, Midjourney $288, Claude.ai $200, Anthropic $200 and Kling $79 — totalling $1,182, or 76% of all AI spending. Four of the five landed in a six-week window between mid-January and early March.

Recommendation Confidence is moderate: some of these may be annual plans that simply haven't renewed yet, and July is already trending back up. Budget AI as a ~$500/year experimentation allowance rather than a monthly line — that matches how you actually buy it.
High confidence

The hotel room is only 71% of the hotel bill

Marriott is your single largest merchant at $2,509 — ahead of the power company, the dentist and the airline. But only $1,795 of that is lodging. The other $714 is on-property food and beverage, billed separately at JW Orlando and Sanibel Harbour. Every $100 of room rate carried an extra $40 of resort dining.

Recommendation When budgeting a trip, multiply the room rate by 1.4 rather than adding a separate meals line — that single adjustment would have predicted your actual travel cost within a few percent on all four trips.
Strong signal

The weekend doesn't end on Sunday night

Saturday and Sunday carry $14,909 — 37.3% of the year on 29% of the calendar, or 1.49× a typical weekday. That much is expected. What isn't: Monday is the third-heaviest day at $6,177, while Tuesday is the lightest at $3,625. The elevated-spending window is three days long, not two — and 75% of all restaurant dollars fall inside it.

Recommendation If you set a discretionary cap, scope it Saturday-through-Monday rather than to the weekend. A Tuesday-anchored weekly reset would sit at the natural low point in your own cycle.
