The four core views of Tesoro, the budget category taxonomy, and the AI prompts that initialize each section on page load.

Main Functions

Four Core Views

01

Financial Brief

Overall synopsis of financial activity in the data range — the home screen, auto-generated on load. A 3–5 sentence narrative with a time-of-day greeting.

Auto-generates Home screen LLM narrative
02

Net Worth

Dashboard and analysis of overall financial picture. Assets minus liabilities. Visualizes the full holdings snapshot across all loaded data sources.

Assets Liabilities Dashboard
03

Investments

Dashboard and analysis of the investment portfolio. Breakdowns by stocks/equities, Bitcoin, and property holdings.

Stocks Bitcoin Property
04

Spending

Dashboard and analysis of spending across cash and credit accounts. Rich graphical charts with outlier callouts. Timeframe toggle: 1w · 1mo · 3mo · YTD.

1w 1mo 3mo YTD

Mastro Prompts

Page-Load Instructions

Budget — on load

"Organize the available data into the categories below and display a rich table-style view of an average month in each category according to the data."

Financial Brief — on load

"Look at the data provided and give a brief summary about the financial activity you observe. Include greeting according to what the time of day is — e.g. 'Good Morning.' A 3–5 sentence summary."

Net Worth — on load

"Generate a dashboard and give analysis of the overall financial picture in a statement at the top of the page. Then create a dashboard-style graphic showing assets and liabilities in the data set."

Spending — on load

"Generate a dashboard of spending across cash and credit accounts. Give insights about anything that stands out as an outlier. The goal is to put spending into perspective with rich graphic charts and items that seem out of the ordinary."

Investments — on load

"Generate a dashboard of investment portfolio, breakdown by stocks, Bitcoin, and property."
