Mastro · Analysis

Travel is the only variable that moves the bill

Non-travel months — Oct, Dec, Jan, Mar — held flat between $4,897 and $5,443. A spread of $546 across four months. Nov added Ritz-Carlton Naples ($1,846) and Delta ($1,285). Feb added Marriott Aspen ($2,124), United ($986), and ski concierge ($429). Strip those charges out and both months land squarely inside the baseline band.

Baseline discretionary behavior is stable. The budget risk here is not gradual creep — it is the frequency and cost of unbudgeted travel events. Two trips in six months added approximately $7,200 above baseline. At this pace, travel alone represents $14,400/year in incremental spend above a $69,900 annualized run rate.

Recommendation

Set a dedicated travel reserve: $2,500–3,500 per trip, funded monthly at $500–600. With baseline this stable, the math closes easily. The only uncertainty is trip frequency.

Mastro · Analysis

Felix Trattoria is a recurring cost disguised as social spending

11 visits across every month of the 6-month period. $2,024 total. $184 average per visit. No other restaurant in the data reaches 40% of Felix's visit frequency. In January alone — a non-travel, non-holiday month — there were 4 visits totaling $665. That single month exceeds the entire grocery bill at Bristol Farms for the same period.

High-frequency, mid-ticket dining at one venue compounds invisibly. At $184/visit and the current 1.8 visits per month pace, this is a $3,979/year line item that reads as spontaneous in the moment. The pattern suggests Felix functions as a default destination — the choice when there is no specific plan. Annualized, it is the 5th largest single-vendor spend on this card.

Recommendation

Name it: decide how many Felix visits per month are intentional. Even reducing from ~2 to ~1.5 saves $440 annually. The goal is awareness, not restriction.

Mastro · Analysis

Amazon has a post-payment window pattern

Of 9 Amazon charges in this period, 7 averaged $177. The two outliers — Oct 27 at $1,327 and Dec 27 at $1,224 — averaged $1,275 and combined to $2,551. Both occurred within days of a prior autopay clearing. In October, autopay cleared on Nov 17; the $1,327 charge was placed Oct 27. In December, autopay cleared Dec 15; the $1,224 charge was placed Dec 27.

This timing is consistent with a known behavioral pattern: large discretionary purchases feel lower-stakes when the running balance has recently reset or is about to reset. The $2,551 combined total exceeds every individual month's grocery spend and represents 7.3% of six-month net spend. Confidence in the timing pattern is moderate — two instances, consistent positioning.

Recommendation

Add a 24-hour pause on any single Amazon charge above $400. If the purchase was planned, the delay costs nothing. If it was not, the pause usually reveals that.
